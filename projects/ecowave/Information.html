Why pollution matters

Understand the three major forms of pollution and explore a few resources for learning more.

Air pollution

Vehicle emissions, industrial activity, and burning fossil fuels can release pollutants that harm human health and ecosystems.

Water pollution

Harmful substances and waste can enter rivers, lakes, and oceans, affecting wildlife, ecosystems, and human communities.

Land pollution

Littering, improper waste disposal, and habitat destruction can damage soil, ecosystems, and surrounding waterways.
